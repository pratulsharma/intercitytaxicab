- After each day, spend 3 minutes on the daily log (section 2).
- On every review day, update the weekly summary (section 3) and the skills matrix (section 4).
- Practise interview questions aloud. Tick a question only when you can answer it in under a minute **without looking** (sections 6 to 8).

**Confidence scale:** 1 = lost · 2 = shaky · 3 = can do with notes · 4 = can do alone · 5 = can teach it.
